# HuntersWeb
Criminals, haunts, your own family, or entities beyond simple understanding. put a bounty here and we can deal with it for you.

There are Hunters and Mercs in our service that don't know about our services nor are they aware of our influence.

You can reach out yourself or have us reach out for you completely anonymously!

Pick out which hunter you'd like to work with using carefully curated portfolios- or simply put out a bounty and we'll take care of the rest.
Find out schedules and plan accordingly for whoever- or whatever- you need dead.

This website is for an ARG on the Zachobuilds Discord Minecraft server.
